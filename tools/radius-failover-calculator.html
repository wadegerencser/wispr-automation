Formula: timeout × (retransmit + 1) × servers = worst-case seconds a client waits before the WLC falls through to the next method in the AAA method list (e.g. local), per RFC 2865 § 2.5 retry behavior. Default values (5s timeout, 2 retransmits, 1 server) match vars/wispr_radius_vsa_vars.yml in this repo — 15 seconds is a long time for a guest to stare at a captive portal before local fallback auth takes over.
